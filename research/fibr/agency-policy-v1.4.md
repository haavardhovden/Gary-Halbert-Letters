## 📘 Brand manual

[Fibr_brand_mini_manual.pdf](Fibr_brand_mini_manual.pdf)

- **Quick reference**
    - **Logo:** Wordmark only, horizontal only. Always in Fibr red or oat. On a single product it can match that product's colours. Never stretch it or change its colours, and make sure it's readable at small sizes.
    - **Colours:** Red `#e6004b` is the main colour. Oat `#ffdc96` is the second colour. Dark red `#af0537` is the third, plus white and black. Never put red text on dark red, or the other way around. Lighter tints are fine for backgrounds.
    - **Fonts** (free on Adobe Fonts): Market Pro Bold for headlines, Filson Pro Bold/Regular for body text, OCR A for tables and info boxes.
    - **Graphics:** Headlines in Market Pro can be skewed and rotated 5%. Use the oat symbol to highlight USPs and dividers to structure layouts.
    - ⚠️ **Tagline "Fight your cravings":** Don't use it in ads for now. The 2026 policy bans claims about cravings, so we're checking with Fibr first.

---

> 💡 **The short version:** Be factual about the product and real about personal taste. We can be as creative as we want with angles, as long as every fact can be proven and nothing hints at a health effect. **Not sure? Use less text, don't publish, and ask Håvard first.**

> 🎨 **For designers and editors:** Show the real product in the right packaging, exactly as it looks. Never change its colour, texture or size. No AI-made products, packaging or people. Small background retouching is fine.

## Never

- **Never claim or hint at a health effect.** Nothing about gut or digestion, blood sugar, energy, feeling full, cravings, weight, performance, mood, focus, illness or wellbeing. That includes images, emojis or wording that suggest it.
- **Never script a creator's reaction.** We can't write a glowing review and have a creator present it as their own spontaneous opinion. Real reactions are fine.
- **Never use doctors, dietitians, nutritionists, white coats or other "health experts"** to endorse the product.
- **Never change how the product looks.** No edits to colour, texture, size or packaging.
- **Never use AI to make the product, packaging, people, voices or testimonials.** No AI or deepfake-style creators.
- **Never turn one person's opinion into a claim about everyone.** One happy customer ≠ "everyone loves it".

## Always

- **Stick to facts we can prove:** what the product is and what's in it, how it's baked and where it comes from, the format, and when and how to eat it.
- **Be creative with the angle.** Taste and texture, fika and coffee moments, serving ideas, product demos, ingredients, craft and origin, convenience, and fair comparisons we can back up. Same freedom for UGC, video and statics.
- **Keep opinions real.** Creators can share how they genuinely feel, like "I love the taste" or "this is perfect with my coffee", as long as it's true for them.
- **Use real reviews only.** They must be genuine, we must have permission to use them, and they must be quoted exactly.
- **Mark creator content as an ad from the start**, with Fibr shown as the brand behind it. Use the platform's paid partnership label too.
- **Brief every creator and freelancer on these rules.** We are responsible for what goes out, not them.

## Words and claims to avoid

| ❌ Don't say | ✅ Say instead (only if true) |
| --- | --- |
| "No sugar spike", "stable blood sugar" | Facts about what's in it, if Fibr has approved the wording |
| "Good for your gut / stomach" | "Great with coffee", or describe the taste |
| "Keeps you full" | "A perfect fika snack" |
| "Healthy fika", "healthier", "guilt-free", "clean" | Describe taste, texture, ingredients or origin |
| "The best", "everyone loves it", "tastes exactly like the original" | "I love the taste" (the creator's own real opinion) |

**Nutrition claims** (e.g. about fibre or sugar content) can only be used when Fibr has approved **that exact wording for that exact product**. Check before using it.

## Legal or compliance limits

- **One approval ≠ approval for everything.** If Fibr or Meta approved one ad, that doesn't cover new wording elsewhere. New claim or new ad copy = new check.
- **Ads Manager:** Budgets, bids, audiences, placements and moving spend between already approved ads is fine. New claims, new ad copy, or Meta's automatic creative changes (AI text, image tweaks) need a check first.
- **Prices and discounts must be accurate.** A "before" price in a sale must be the lowest price from the last 30 days.
- **Consent and rights:** Every creator or model must have agreed in writing to be used in paid social ads (format, countries and how long). We also need the rights to all footage, photos, music, voice-over and text we use.
- **Swedish and EU food and marketing rules apply.** Fibr can choose to be stricter than the law, and their rules win.
- **When in doubt, don't publish.** Anything about claims, prices, origin, reviews, AI or personal data that isn't clearly OK needs written approval from Fibr first.
