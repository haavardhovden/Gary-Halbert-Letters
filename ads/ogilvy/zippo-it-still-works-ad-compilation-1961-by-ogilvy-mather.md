### Annonse 1 (MacKinlay Kantor)

**Bildetekst:** THE 10-YEAR-OLD ZIPPO IN OUR PICTURE HAS LIGHTED ITS WAY THROUGH MILLIONS OF WORDS. MR. KANTOR STILL USES IT EVERY DAY.

**Headline:** Pulitzer Prize author, MacKinlay Kantor, has used this Zippo lighter since 1951. It still works today.

**Subhead:** No matter how old it is, if your Zippo ever fails to work, we'll fix it free

MacKinlay Kantor's Zippo lighter was already three years old when he started to write his magnificent Civil War novel, "Andersonville," in 1953.

In 1955, the book was published and won a Pulitzer Prize for best novel of the year. Mr. Kantor's Zippo was still working. Every time.

Mr. Kantor is the author of such classics as "The Voice of Bugle Ann" and "Glory For Me," the poignant novel in verse which was the basis for one of the most popular movies of all time, "The Best Years Of Our Lives."

In 1956, Mr. Kantor began writing his major novel "Spirit Lake," which has just appeared. As you can see in our picture, his Zippo lighter is still working today.

Ten-, fifteen-, and twenty-five-year-old Zippos that still work are anything but rare.

### Annonse 2 (Lou Little)

**Bildetekst:** OUR PICTURE SHOWS HOW THE UNCOMPLETED ROSE BOWL LOOKED IN THE 1930'S. LOU'S ZIPPO WORKED THE WETTEST GAME IN ROSE BOWL HISTORY

**Headline:** Lou Little used this Zippo lighter when Columbia played in the Rose Bowl Game of 1934. It still works today.

**Subhead:** No matter how old it is, if your Zippo ever fails to work, we'll fix it free.

If ever a lighter was put to a test, it was the Zippo Lou Little used during the Rose Bowl Game in 1934.

The rain came down in torrents. The field was a solid sheet of water. Lou smoked a number of cigarettes. The lighter worked every time.

The game is now a football classic. Lou Little has treasured his Zippo ever since.

During his 27 years as coach at Columbia, Lou fielded some great teams. He was equally famous for his interest in the scholastic achievements of his players. A former dean of Columbia College once remarked, "Perhaps some of the best teaching in the University is being done these days by Mr. Little, at Baker Field."

When Lou retired at the end of 1956, he was the dean of American football coaches. The Zippo lighter he used in 1934 still works today.

### Annonse 3 (Ernie Pyle)

**Bildetekst:** THE BATTLE-MARKED ZIPPO IN OUR STORY BELONGS TO DONALD C. HYDE, BIRMINGHAM, MICH. HE STILL USES IT EVERY DAY.

**Headline:** Ernie Pyle scratched his shortest war story on this Zippo lighter in 1945. It still works today.

**Subhead:** No matter how old it is, if your Zippo ever fails to work, we'll fix it free

TIME: March, 1945. PLACE: Aboard carrier USS Cabot, CVL-28, under way, South Pacific.

Ernie Pyle was aboard. The scuttlebutt was that something big was coming.

A half hour before operation orders were to be opened, a young officer was pumping Ernie Pyle to find out where the ship was headed.

Ernie wasn't talking. He asked the officer for his Zippo lighter. With a knife, Ernie scratched something on the bottom of the lighter. "Stick this in your pocket," Ernie said, "and promise not to look until the orders are opened."

With the first blast of the bosun's pipe for attention to orders, the young officer took the Zippo from his pocket. Scratched on the bottom of the lighter was the word, Tokyo.

The first all-out carrier assault on the Japanese mainland had begun.

## Bildet

Alle tre: en hånd holder den tente lighteren i forgrunnen, med et miljø som forankrer historien bak (bokrygger med "Andersonville, Pulitzer Prize Novel", Rose Bowl-stadion, en slitt lighter med inngravering). Lighteren i bildet er den fra historien. Bildet gjør jobben som bevis, og bildeteksten forsterker det: "still uses it every day".

## Struktur

1. Oppmerksomhet: "Ernie Pyle scratched his shortest war story on this Zippo lighter in 1945." Kjent navn pluss en kuriositet.
2. Hvorfor bry seg: "It still works today."
3. Hvorfor tro: "No matter how old it is, if your Zippo ever fails to work, we'll fix it free."
4. Bevis: historien i brødteksten, bildet, og bildeteksten med eierens navn og by ("DONALD C. HYDE, BIRMINGHAM, MICH.").
5. Fordeler: "The lighter worked every time." Én fordel, holdbarhet, i tre varianter.
6. Hvordan bestille: produktrekke med priser. Ingen adresse ut over produsenten.
7. Nå: mangler.

Headline-type: sitat/spesifikk detalj i historieform. Mal: [kjent person] + [hendelse] + [årstall] + "It still works today."

Bevissthetsgrad: produktbevisst.

## Strategi og psykologi

Strategi: samme mal som 1964, tre år tidligere, med lange tekster. I 1961 måtte historien fortelles, ingen visste hva "It still works" betydde. I 1964 holdt navn og årstall. Malen ble kortere etter hvert som publikum ble varmere.
Psykologi: kjendisen daterer, han anbefaler ikke. 1951, Pulitzer 1955, lighteren står på hele tidslinja. "Anything but rare" gjør historien til regel i stedet for unntak. Bildet av uferdig Rose Bowl daterer like mye som teksten.
Lærdom: en serie starter lang og blir kort. Ikke kutt teksten før publikum kjenner løftet, ikke behold den etterpå. → #74.

## Vurdering

- **Les:** delvis. Les Ernie Pyle-teksten i sin helhet, den er en komplett historie på under 150 ord. Skum de to andre.
- **Oppsummering:** Tre annonser fra 1961 med samme skjelett som 1964-serien, men med lengre brødtekst. Hver forteller historien om én bestemt lighter (forfatter, fotballtrener, krigskorrespondent), gjentar at den fortsatt virker, og lover gratis reparasjon. Produktrekken er lik i alle tre.
- **Verdi:** ja. Ernie Pyle-teksten er et skolebokeksempel på en historie som selger uten å pitche: produktet er rekvisitt i en god historie, og løftet (holdbarhet) blir konklusjonen leseren trekker selv. Dette er hvordan man skriver en UGC-historie i 2026.
- **Mekanisme:** #19 spesifisitet, #42 interessant fakta + segue (historien først, produktet etterpå), #57 Star, Story, Solution, #68 garanti. Kandidat til ny mekanisme: nei. Serie-malen er allerede foreslått fra 1964-samlingen; denne er samme mønster med lengre tekst.
- **Stjel:**
  1. "TIME: March, 1945. PLACE: Aboard carrier USS Cabot." Åpne en historie med tid og sted som stikkord. Ingen innledning. Fungerer som første linje i primærtekst.
  2. Bildeteksten er der beviset bor: fullt navn, by, "still uses it every day". I paid social er det captionen under UGC-bildet. Sett navn og sted der, ikke "fornøyd kunde".
  3. Skriv historien slik at produktet aldri er hovedperson. Lighteren er rekvisitt. Løftet står bare i headline og garanti.
