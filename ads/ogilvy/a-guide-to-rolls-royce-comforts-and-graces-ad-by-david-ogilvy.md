# A guide to Rolls-Royce comforts and graces

## Tekst (transkribert fra bildet)

**Headline:** A guide to Rolls-Royce comforts and graces

**Subhead:** The Best Car in the World eschews useless gadgetry. It does, however, minister royally to a civilized taste for creature comforts.

THE Rolls-Royce and its fraternal twin, the Bentley, are designed for people who are determined to live well while in transit. There are few *reasonable* desires which the owner cannot satisfy by pressing a button, pulling a lever, or flicking a switch.

For example, if the road surface is not to your liking, you can, in effect, change it. A switch on the steering [tegning: ratt] column lets you adjust the shock absorbers to suit driving conditions. You harden or soften the ride at will.

"Rough roads pass almost unnoticed," writes Gordon Wilkins, motoring correspondent of *The Observer*, "for they neither shock the occupants nor assail their ears with drumming noises."

If the noise from *other* cars distresses you, you can close the windows and still get fresh air through an efficient ventilating system. Within your Rolls-Royce, blessed silence reigns. You can converse in discreet whispers.

Three walnut picnic tables are among the graces of the Rolls-Royce [tegning: piknikbord med kopp] and Bentley. One slides out from the dash—an ideal place for a road map. Two others fold down behind the front seats. They make fine work surfaces upon which the children can rest their coloring books to keep them quietly amused on long trips.

The rake of the front seats is *individually* adjustable. The driver can sit erect for a commanding view of the road while his companion reclines. Each has his own set of arm-rests which adjust to suit his fancy.

The seats are as cozy as club chairs. You *nestle* into them. They are cov[tegning: seter]ered with the same English leather used on seats in the House of Commons. Eight hides are used—enough to make 128 pairs of shoes.

There are two illuminated vanity mirrors in niches in the rear quarters of the Rolls-Royce. Ladies may thus [tegning: speil] perfect their make-up in privacy before leaving the car.

Here you see the rear window defroster. Or, rather, you do *not* see it. It consists of a heating element of 1,360 *nearly invisible* wires moulded [tegning: bakrute] into the glass—wires finer than a human hair. At the flick of a switch, mist and frost are cleared electrically.

Much of the interior is surfaced with French walnut which has been hand-polished by English craftsmen. Notes are made on the hue and burl of the walnut used in each car, and are filed under your serial number. If a portion of the wood should ever be damaged, it can be matched flawlessly in grain and finish.

**Bildetekst:** The Rolls-Royce Silver Cloud II—$14,895 P.O.E.

The gas tank cannot be opened without the driver's consent. *You* unlock it electrically from a button on the dash. And you should never run out of gas. A green light on the instrument panel starts winking when your supply dwindles to the last three gallons.

Your luggage rides here in carpeted seclusion. It can never be soiled by the spare wheel, which occupies a *separate* [tegning: bagasjerom] compartment under the luggage. You can test the air pressure in the spare through a special port without removing the tire.

Under a hatch in the wheel compartment you will find an assortment of cadmium-plated tools and replacement bulbs. They rest in a fitted case of *sponge rubber*—further evidence of Rolls-Royce's obsession with peace and quiet.

Corrosion is held at bay by a plating of chromium or cadmium on every nut, bolt, washer and cotter pin. Even the tail pipe is chromed.

A dictating machine, an Espresso coffee maker, an electric razor, a bed, a telephone, and water for washing are available as optional extra conveniences.

**The basic convenience of the Rolls-Royce is, of course, its magnificent riding qualities. Even at 100 m.p.h. (laws permitting) you have a feeling of sublime safety. Yet the 4700-pound car is delicately maneuverable.**

The power-assisted steering is, according to Robert Glenton in the *Sunday Express*, "as delicate as the plop of a pearl into a glass of champagne...." The power brakes will halt the car squarely from 100 m.p.h. without a tremor. And there are *three* independent brake linkages—two hydraulic and one mechanical. In the unlikely event that one linkage should fail, the others automatically keep the car under perfect control.

[tegning: to grillar] Here you see the only difference between the Rolls-Royce and the Bentley—their radiator design. In all other respects they are identical motor cars. The Bentley costs $300 less because its radiator is simpler to make.

If you desire the rewarding experience of driving a Rolls-Royce or Bentley, write Rolls-Royce, Inc., 30 Rockefeller Plaza, New York 20, CIrcle 5-1144, or telephone to one of the dealers listed on page 62.

## Struktur

1. Oppmerksomhet: "A guide to ..." (formatløfte, ikke salgsløfte) og tegningene.
2. Hvorfor bry seg: "designed for people who are determined to live well while in transit."
3. Hvorfor tro: "There are few reasonable desires which the owner cannot satisfy by pressing a button, pulling a lever, or flicking a switch."
4. Bevis: 1,360 tråder i bakruta, åtte huder, 128 par sko, valnøtt arkivert under serienummer, Observer- og Sunday Express-sitater.
5. Fordeler: stillhet, justerbare seter, barn som holder seg rolige, sminkespeil, bagasje som ikke blir skitten.
6. Hvordan bestille: "write Rolls-Royce, Inc., 30 Rockefeller Plaza ... or telephone to one of the dealers listed on page 62."
7. Nå: mangler.

Headline-type: kuriositet/format ("guide"). Ingen løfte, ingen nyhet. Subhead gjør posisjoneringsjobben (ikke gadgets, men komfort).
Bevissthetsgrad: produktbevisst. Skrevet til folk som allerede vurderer bilen og vil vite hvordan det er å sitte i den.

## Vurdering

- **Les:** skum. Se på layouten (tegning ved siden av hver funksjon) og les avsnittet om valnøtt og serienummer.
- **Oppsummering:** Annonsen går gjennom komforten i Rolls-Royce som en illustrert guide: justerbare støtdempere, piknikbord, individuelle seter, sminkespeil, usynlig bakrutevarme, valnøtt som arkiveres per bil, tanklokk som låses fra dashbordet, bagasjerom uten reservehjul, verktøy i skumgummi. Den avslutter med kjøreegenskaper, forskjellen på Rolls og Bentley, og forhandlerhenvisning.
- **Verdi:** ja, for formatet. "Guide" i stedet for "annonse" gir tillatelse til å liste ti funksjoner uten at det føles som mas. Tegning pluss avsnitt er en karusell på papir.
- **Mekanisme:** #19 spesifisitet, #36 "picture with pleasure" (leseren settes i setet: "You nestle into them", "You can converse in discreet whispers", barna med fargebøker), #16 advertorial-format (ser ut som redaksjonell guide). Kandidat til ny mekanisme: nei.
- **Stjel:**
  1. Karusell som "guide til X": ett kort per funksjon, tegning eller nærbilde pluss to setninger. Vidda: "En guide til hva som skjer under setet" med ett kort per detalj (lås, stoff, vekt, bæresele).
  2. Skriv funksjonen som en scene med leseren i, ikke som spesifikasjon. "Barna kan hvile fargebøkene på bordet" selger bordet bedre enn "tre piknikbord i valnøtt".
  3. Detaljen som viser omsorg ingen ba om (verktøy i skumgummi, valnøtt arkivert på serienummer). Én slik detalj gjør hele produktet troverdig.
