## Tekst (hovedinnhold, transkribert fra bildet)

**Headline:** An Open Letter To Every Attractive Woman In Miami Who Is Serious About Having A Career As A Professional Model

DATELINE: South Miami Beach. If you are already a professional model or, if you'd like to be, this is going to be the most exciting message you will ever read. On the other hand, if you want to be a model... but... you don't really have what it takes... this message can save you thousands of dollars and a great deal of misery and heartbreak.

Top fashion models: only a handful in the world, several million a year, own companies. **Do You Have What It Takes To Become A Top Fashion Model?** Probably not. Statistically, only about one out of every 37,512 women have the necessary qualifications: 1. drop dead good-looking. 2. extremely photogenic, the camera must *love* you (many beautiful women look ordinary in photographs, plain women come out spectacular, *nobody knows why*). 3. tall and lean, at least five feet eight, no extra body fat (photographs add ten pounds and take two inches). 4. **Luck!** The top model has, for some reason, gotten more exposure and publicity. Odd fact: a cover model for Elle or Vogue sometimes gets paid as little as $50. Why? Because **within 15 minutes after the magazine hits the street** her phone rings off the hook. Exposure is everything.

**You Do Not Have To Be A "Rail" To Have A Successful Modeling Career!** There are thousands of other assignments that pay very well. True story: an accountant who ran "How To Pay Zero Taxes" ads in the Wall Street Journal. A photographer friend put a gorgeous young blond woman in the ad, just standing there. **That Ad Made More Money For The Accountant Than Any Other Ad He Ever Ran!** Sex sells. But it has to be done with good taste. There are thousands of businesses which could make more profit if only **They Knew How To Use Attractive Models In Their Marketing Campaigns!** Most business owners don't know how effective it is, and don't know how to find a model or arrange a shoot. **Most Advertising Agencies Don't Have A Clue How To Do It!** So there are thousands of models who want work and tens of thousands of businesses who should use them, and no one to connect them.

There is now a solution. But first, what you should *never* do:
**#1: Don't Waste Your Money Going To A Modeling "School"!** Most are scams. They will tell you you have a shot *no matter what you look like*. Send an overweight, plain friend and we'll bet someone tells her she has "a certain look".
**#2: Don't Waste Your Money To Pay Some Magazine To Put Your Photographs In Their Publication!** Nobody who counts ever looks at these magazines.
**#3: Don't Waste Your Time And Money Dealing With "Fake" Modeling Agencies!** "New Faces Needed", "Giant Modeling Talent Hunt At XYZ Hotel". Hundreds show up, get a lecture, and are sold a "support team". It's all lies. A total scam.

**What you should do:** Get out the yellow pages of the Miami telephone book and turn to page 1557. Under "Modeling Agencies" there are 59 of them. Call, ask for an appointment, show up on time and say **"I Want To Be A Model. Do I Have What It Takes?"** They will say no (gently) or maybe. If maybe, a test shoot. **If they are legitimate and really interested in you, they won't ask you to pay a dime for anything.**

**Why You Should Not Feel Bad If The Modeling Agencies Reject You!** Many of the most attractive women don't photograph half as well as they look. Agencies specialize. Modeling is a business. You are still an extremely beautiful woman and a unique and valuable human being.

**Here Is The Best News Of All!** South Miami Beach is the most exciting place on earth, becoming the center of the modeling universe. Last year (1993) 5,600 professional models were photographed here, 1.5 million dollars a day. There is a new publication called *Charisma*, published from South Beach, offices next to the News Cafe, Penthouse Suite at 820 Ocean Drive. A monthly newsletter with no advertising, to all 50 states and 39 countries. Not free: business owners and agencies pay serious money to subscribe. Charisma publishes photos and details about the most exciting new models we can find. When a subscriber decides she is right for his advertising, he calls us, we tell him how to contact her. We do *not* charge for this. We do *not* take a cut. We have no desire to become an agency. We do *not* charge a fee to put someone in the newsletter. You cannot buy your way in. Actually, we *pay* a generous fee to every model we use.

**You Won't Believe How Good Our People Are!** Photographers Kevin Halbert and Natalie Boehm, trained at the Design Center in Pasadena, obsessed with their work. Natalie once spent three days on a 1957 Corvette **Just To Get The Lighting Right!** Those photos will make you look better than any photos of yourself that have ever been taken.

How does Charisma make a profit? 1. Subscribers pay a lot for the newsletter. 2. Photos of the "Charisma Girls" will be used in posters and calendars, and the girls get a healthy commission.

**CTA:** Do *not* send us any photos. Do *not* drop by our Ocean Drive offices. *Call* us and give a secretary your name and mailing address. We will send you a free copy of Charisma plus a fact sheet revealing how much we pay our models. The free issue features Jennifer Driver, one of the new Guess? jeans models. Then, if you are still interested, call for an appointment or send a few photographs (amateur shots or polaroids are OK). The only unique qualification: you are *not* already famous. We are far too low on the food chain to be of any value to Cindy Crawford. What we want is to be the first publication to recognize and publicize **The Top Models Of The Future!** Office hours 9 to 5, Monday through Friday. **(800) 327-0028.** Hey, thanks for your attention and God bless you, whatever you decide to do.

## Value og key takeaways

**Value:** Rådgiveren som advarer. Halbert bruker to tredjedeler av annonsen på å fortelle henne hva hun ikke skal gjøre og hvorfor hun sannsynligvis ikke har det som trengs. Da tror hun på ham når han sier hva hun skal gjøre. Og det han selger er gratis, med en forklaring på hvordan han tjener penger likevel.

1. Åpent brev til én bestemt person. "Every attractive woman in Miami who is serious about..." Callout, sted, og et krav (serious). Alle andre går.
2. "Probably not." Han sier hun ikke har det som trengs. 1 av 37 512. Ærligheten er hooken.
3. Advarslene bygger tilliten. Tre svindler han avslører, med detaljer hun kjenner igjen ("New Faces Needed"). Etter det er han på hennes side.
4. Gi bort metoden. Gule sider, side 1557, 59 byråer, hva du skal si. Hun trenger ikke ham for å bli modell. Derfor tror hun på resten (#72).
5. Utvid markedet. "You do not have to be a rail." Regnskapsføreren og den blonde damen. Nå er hun kvalifisert likevel.
6. Forklar hvordan vi tjener penger. Abonnenter og plakater. Gratis uten grunn er en felle. Gratis med regnskap er et tilbud.
7. Bevis for kvalitet med én detalj. Tre dager på en Corvette for lyset.
8. Instruksjon med nei: ikke send bilder, ikke kom innom. Ring. Ett lite steg.
9. "God bless you, whatever you decide to do." Ingen push. Han har allerede vunnet henne.

Kort sagt: fortell henne sannheten, advar henne mot de andre, gi bort metoden, og forklar hvorfor du kan være gratis.

## Løfter og closing

Løfter: gratis eksemplar av Charisma, faktaark om hva vi betaler, bilder som gjør deg bedre enn noen gang, vi betaler deg, vi tar ikke en andel, du kan ikke kjøpe deg inn, vi vil være den første som oppdager deg.

Closing: ring (ikke send, ikke kom), sekretæren tar navn og adresse, gratis blad, så bestemmer du. Åpningstider, nummer, velsignelse. Ingen frist, ingen knapphet. Tilliten er closingen.

## Vurdering

- **Les:** les. Hele. Den lengste og mest ærlige av Halberts annonser.
- **Oppsummering:** Halbert skriver til kvinner i Miami som vil bli modeller, sier de fleste ikke har det som trengs, advarer mot tre svindler, forklarer hvordan de kan finne ekte byråer selv, og presenterer Charisma: et nyhetsbrev som viser nye modeller til annonsører, gratis for modellen, betalt av abonnentene.
- **Verdi:** ja. "Rådgiveren som advarer" som tillitsstruktur, og reason-why for gratis i ren form.
- **Mekanisme:** #35 be om et nei ("probably not"), #64 insider som avslører bransjen, #72 gi bort metoden, #17-familien reason-why, #70 to-stegs, #43 instruksjon. Ingen ny.
- **Stjel:** For Stille: "Tre ting du ikke skal kjøpe mot nattesvette" som advertorial. Gel-topper, kjølepute, "kjølende" mikrofiber. Så: hva som faktisk hjelper, og hvorfor vi kan si det.
