### Annonse 1

**Bilde:** Gråhåret mann med pipe, smiler, fisker fra en båt.
**Headline:** To men who want to Quit Work some day

THIS PAGE is addressed to those thousands of earnest, hard-working men who want to take things easier some day. It tells how these men, by following a simple, definite plan, can provide for themselves in later years *a guaranteed income they cannot outlive*.

**How the Plan Works.** It doesn't matter whether your present income is large or merely average. It doesn't matter whether you are making fifty dollars a week or five hundred. If you follow this plan you will some day have an income upon which to retire. The plan calls for the deposit of only a few dollars each month. The minute you make your first deposit, your biggest money worries begin to disappear. Even if you should become totally and permanently disabled, you would not need to worry.

**Boks: New Retirement Income Plan.** It guarantees when you are 65: a monthly income for life of $100 ... or a cash settlement of $12,000. It guarantees upon death from any natural cause before 65 ... It guarantees upon death resulting from accident before 60 ... It guarantees throughout permanent total disability ...

**Get this free book.** The Phoenix Mutual Company is a 125 million dollar company. For over three-quarters of a century it has been helping thousands of men and women to end money worries. But you're not interested in us. You are interested in what we can do for *you*. An illustrated 36-page book called "How to Get the Things You Want" tells you exactly that. This financial plan is simple, reasonable, and logical. Not for failures, not for people who can't make ends meet, but for hard-working, forward-looking people who know what they want and are ready to make definite plans to get it. No obligation. Get your copy of the book now.

Kupong: Send me by mail without obligation, your new book "How to Get the Things You Want." Name, date of birth, business address, home address.

### Annonse 2

**Bilde:** Smilende mann i stol med pipe.
**Headline:** How you can retire on $200 a month

THIS new Retirement Income Plan enables you to provide for yourself a guaranteed income for life. The income begins at any age you say, 55, 60 or 65. It can be any amount you wish, $100 a month, $200, $300 or more. Unconditionally guaranteed by the Phoenix Mutual, a 79-year-old company with assets of over 135 million dollars.

**Spørsmål og svar:** A business man recently asked us questions about the Plan. "A Retirement Income takes care of me, but suppose my wife outlives me?" "The income can be made to continue as long as either you or your wife lives." "Suppose I should be killed suddenly by some accidental means?" "Your wife would receive double the amount." "Suppose serious illness or accident should destroy my earning power while I was still young?" "The company would pay you a monthly income as long as your disability lasted." "How much does a Retirement Income cost?" "That depends upon your present age and the amount of income you want to retire on. It is purchased on the installment plan. This makes the payments comparatively small."

**Send for the Facts.** An interesting 28-page book tells how you can provide a Retirement Income for yourself, money to send your son to college, money to leave your home free of debt. Send for your copy now. No cost. No obligation.

## Value og key takeaways

**Value:** Samme produkt, samme bilde av en fornøyd mann med pipe, tre headlines. Caples testet headlinen, ikke resten. "To men who want to quit work some day" er den som vant og gikk i årevis. Alt annet i annonsen er der for å levere det headlinen lover.

1. Callout pluss ønske i én linje. "To men who want to quit work some day." Hvem den er til, og hva de vil. Ikke "pensjonsforsikring".
2. Bildet er dream outcome, ikke produktet. Mannen fisker. Ingen polise, ingen bank. Hun ser livet etter kjøpet.
3. Tallet i headlinen. "$200 a month" gjør drømmen konkret. Testet mot ord, og tall vinner ofte.
4. "You're not interested in us. You are interested in what we can do for you." Han sier det rett ut, og bytter fra selskapet til leseren.
5. Innvendingene som spørsmål og svar. "Suppose my wife outlives me?" Tvileren i teksten, 45 år før Ogilvy skrev om det.
6. Negativ kvalifisering. "Not for failures, not for people who can't make ends meet." Den rette leseren blir stolt.
7. Selg boka, ikke polisen. Gratis, uten forpliktelse. Annonsen selger neste steg (#70).
8. Garanti-tabellen er beviset. Tall i boks, ikke adjektiver.

Kort sagt: headline med hvem og hva de vil, bilde av livet etterpå, og selg bare neste steg.

## Vurdering

- **Les:** les annonse 1. Skum 2 og 3 for å se hva som ble byttet.
- **Oppsummering:** Tre Phoenix Mutual-annonser fra 1929–31 med samme struktur: headline med ønske, bilde av fornøyd mann, plan med garantitabell, gratis bok som CTA. Annonse 2 bruker tall i headline og spørsmål/svar. Annonse 3 legger til "6 months' vacation twice a year".
- **Verdi:** ja. Callout-headlinen, dream outcome i bildet og to-stegs-salget er hele Halbert-skjelettet i 1929.
- **Mekanisme:** #70 to-stegs-annonsen, #77 tvileren og den troende (Q&A-formen), #36 picture with pleasure (fisketuren), negativ kvalifisering, #68 garanti. Ingen ny.
- **Stjel:** "Til deg i overgangsalderen som vil sove tørt igjen" er Caples-formen: hvem, pluss det de vil. Og bildet: ikke lakenet, kvinnen som sover.
