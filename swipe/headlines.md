# Headlines — swipe file

Format: headline → kilde → hvorfor den virker (mek. #) → Stille/Vidda-vri (valgfritt)

## Fra de ni klassiske annonsene (ads/)
- "At 60 miles an hour the loudest noise in this new Rolls-Royce comes from the electric clock" — Ogilvy. Spesifikk detalj som BEVIS, ikke påstand (#19).
- "Do You Make These Mistakes In English?" — Cody. Selvtest + frykt for å dumme seg ut (#15, selvtest-mek.).
- "Why Men Crack" — Postum. Uventet synder → mekanisme → produktet eier logikken (#11).
- "The Lazy Man's Way To Riches" — Karbo. Innrømmer flaw, snakker til den late (#14).
- "How To Collect From Social Security At Any Age" — Halbert. Umulig-løfte med reason-why (#20).
- "How To Burn Off Body Fat, Hour By Hour" — Halbert. Mekanisme i headlinen (#18).
- Coat of Arms-brevet: ingen headline, bare "Dear Mr. Noble" — personalisering ER hooken (#17).

## Fra Halberts egne nyhetsbrev-titler (de er headlines)
- "I Have Approximately The Same Talent For Management That A Pig Has For Solving Geometry Problems!" — selvironi + bilde.
- "An Open Letter To Anyone So Insane That He Wants To Become A Gary Halbert Client" — negativ kvalifisering som headline.
- "Proof That Gary Halbert Is Really Rather Stupid" — feil brukt som hook (letter-115).
- "Save The World... Kill Yourself Now!" — sjokk + kontrast.
- "Let's Give Castration A Chance!" — mønsterbrudd; virker bare med etablert persona.
- "Miami Beach Lesbian Creates New Product That Makes All Women Almost Instantly Crazy To Have Sex With Males... Or... Females!" — letter-124. Nyhet + spesifikk person + forbudt frukt.
- "The Amazing L.A. Roadmap To Real Estate Riches!" — Boron kap. 10. Sted = relevans + troverdighet.
- "How Architects Can Make Extra Money" — Boron kap. 9. Callout-mal: [gruppe] + [løfte].
- "Free Report Reveals... How To Make Up To 15 Profitable Trades Every Single Day The Market Is Open!" — letter-105. Tall + "free report" = lead-gen.
- "Does Size Really Matter? (Finally, Gary Halbert gives you the real answer!)" — letter-118. Spørsmål + omdefinering.
- "Shouldn't this be against the law?" — pre-head fra letter-124. Én linje som skaper mistanke om noe forbudt.

## Fra Boron Letters (mønstre, ikke ferdige headlines)
- "How To Buy L.A. Real Estate With No Money Down" > "How To Buy Real Estate With No Money Down" — legg til ETT spesifikt ord.
- "Dear Bond, here is a great new way for 16 year old kids to make money" — identitet i første linje.
- "Did you know there are only 117 So & So families in the entire U.S.?" — fakta som callout (#40/#42).

- "Sov naken. Sov i bambus." — Jonathans, reverse-engineered fra konkurrent. Resultat: [fyll inn]
